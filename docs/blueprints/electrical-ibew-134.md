## Open question — must be resolved before this exam is built

The project brief (from the user's earlier research) states that Local 134
uses the **GAN battery** while Local 701 uses the ETA/NJATC two-section
format. Re-checking in Phase 0 did **not** confirm the GAN claim, and found
evidence pointing the other way. Both directions have real support:

**Evidence that 134 uses the ETA test**
- IBEW and NECA jointly operate the Electrical Training Alliance, which
  publishes the 33/46 + 36/51 two-section test used by IBEW/NECA JATCs.
- Third-party summaries of the EJATT process describe the Algebra and
  Functions (33 q / 46 min) + Reading Comprehension (36 q / 51 min) format
  and the 1-9 stanine, and describe EJATT as an IBEW 134 + NECA trust.

**Evidence that 134 might use GAN**
- GAN Human Resources (Bloomingdale, IL) states on its own site that it
  serves "more than 50" Chicagoland apprenticeship programs and explicitly
  lists **"electricians"** among the trades it tests, alongside bricklayers,
  tile-setters, plumbers, pipe-fitters, operating engineers, carpenters,
  roofers, sheet metal and service employees.
- EJATT's own FAQ says applicants "will be given a study guide for our
  aptitude test" at registration. GAN sells a $20 study guide at application
  time; the ETA sample test is published free online. A handed-out study
  guide is more consistent with the GAN model.

**Not resolved by:** EJATT's public FAQ, the IN-TECH homepage, and the EJATT
registration PDF, none of which name the testing organization or the format.
The claim that EJATT tests "through the Electrical Training Alliance" traces
to a third-party job aggregator, not to EJATT.

**Resolution path:** ask EJATT directly (708-389-1340 /
Registration@in-techonline.org) or read the study guide handed out at
registration. Until then this stays PARTIAL and the site will say so.
