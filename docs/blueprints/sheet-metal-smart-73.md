# Blueprint: Sheet Metal — SMART Local 73 (Chicago / Cook / Lake)

- **Test name / provider:** Not named publicly by the local. Most likely the
  **GAN Aptitude Battery** (GAN lists "sheet metal" among the Chicagoland
  trades it tests).
- **Locals using it:** Sheet Metal Workers' Local 73 (Bellwood, IL), member of
  the Chicago & Cook County Building & Construction Trades Council.
  SMART Local 265 (Carol Stream) is the other Chicago-area sheet metal local.
- **Overall:** ~140 questions / ~119-120 minutes *if GAN* — unconfirmed.
- **Calculator:** NO (assumed; GAN allows none)
- **Confidence:** PARTIAL
- **Date checked:** 2026-09-21

## Section table

If GAN, the section table is the shared one in
`docs/blueprints/_gan-battery.md` (Reading, Numerical Computation, Numerical
Reasoning, Problem Solving, Bennett Mechanical Aptitude, Spatial Reasoning).

Third-party sheet-metal-specific prep sources describe up to six areas:
Mathematics (basic and advanced), Reading Comprehension, Mechanical
Reasoning, Spatial Relations (shape folding, 2D/3D visualization), and
Abstract Reasoning (pattern recognition, sequence identification). That maps
cleanly onto the GAN battery except for "Abstract Reasoning", which may be
this trade's name for Numerical Reasoning / number series.

## Scoring notes

Local 73's official apprenticeship notice states only: "An apprentice
applicant will take an aptitude test. Applicants will be ranked by test
scores." Passing threshold commonly reported as 70%; ranking is what matters.

## Sources

- SMART Local 73 official apprenticeship opportunity notice (Bellwood, IL):
  confirms an aptitude test exists and that applicants are ranked by score;
  gives no format. Also: min age 17, $20 registration fee, HS diploma/GED,
  drug test and physical, 4-year term.
- https://www.smw73.org/training/application.html — currently "No applications
  are being accepted at this time"; no test format published.
- https://ganhumanresources.com/gan-aptitude-test — lists sheet metal among
  trades GAN tests in Chicagoland.
- https://www.jobtestprep.com/sheetmetal-apprentice-exam — cross-check only.
