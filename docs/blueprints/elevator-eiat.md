# Blueprint: Elevator Constructor — EIAT (NEIEP)

- **Test name / provider:** Elevator Industry Aptitude Test (EIAT) — National
  Elevator Industry Educational Program (NEIEP)
- **Locals using it:** IUEC locals nationally, including IUEC Local 2 (Chicago)
- **Overall:** 100 questions / ~90 minutes
- **Calculator:** NO (paper-and-pencil test)
- **Confidence:** PARTIAL
- **Date checked:** 2026-09-21

## Section table

| # | Section | Questions | Time | Format | Calculator |
|---|---------|-----------|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Reading Comprehension | 35 | 25 min* | Cloze (fill-in-the-blank in passage) | No |
| 2 | Mechanical Comprehension | 35 | 25 min* | Diagram-based multiple choice | No |
| 3 | Arithmetic Computation | 30 | 40 min* | Multiple choice, includes a "No answer" option | No |

\* **Times marked with an asterisk are NOT official.** The NEIEP 2024 study
guide publishes the three sections and the 35/35/30 question split but does
**not** publish per-section time limits. The 25/25/40 split is what prep
providers consistently report and sums to the ~90 minute total. Treated as
derived, not confirmed.

The Arithmetic section's "No answer" choice is a real feature of this format
and is reproduced; it is not used in any other exam in this project.

## Scoring notes

Pass mark 70%. Passing qualifies the applicant to continue; the score also
sets position on the eligibility list, so higher scores are called sooner.
