# Blueprint: GAN Aptitude Battery (shared across many Chicago-area trades)

- **Test name / provider:** GAN Aptitude Battery — GAN Human Resources
  Associates, Bloomingdale, IL
- **Overall:** ~140 questions / ~119-120 minutes
- **Calculator:** NO
- **Confidence:** PARTIAL
- **Date checked:** 2026-09-21

This is the single most important blueprint in the project: GAN tests "more
than 50" Chicagoland apprenticeship programs and roughly 5,000 applicants a
year. Trades whose locals are confirmed or reported to use it are listed
below; each trade's own blueprint references this table.

**The per-section counts and times marked `?` are NOT published by any
official source.** GAN does not publish them; the locals that name the
sections do not give counts or timings. The Reading (42/25) and Problem
Solving (35/35) figures come from third-party prep sites and are treated as
PARTIAL. See "What is unverified".

Section *names* are confirmed from two official union sources (see Sources).
Pipefitters 597 and Plumbers 130 both list five named sections; other
sources describe six, adding Problem Solving. The six-section structure is
what the user's existing exams use.

## Locals confirmed to use GAN

- **UA Local 597** (pipefitters, Chicago) — CONFIRMED, official: "Applicants
  are tested by a third party. (currently GAN Human Resources)."
- **UA Local 130** (plumbers, Chicago) — CONFIRMED: GAN publishes a
  "Plumbers 130 Apprentice Aptitude Test-Taking Tips" page.
- **Iron Workers Local 1** (structural, Chicago) — CONFIRMED: aptitude test
  registration is sold through GAN's own store.
- Bricklayers, tile-setters, operating engineers, carpenters, roofers, sheet
  metal, service employees, electricians — listed by trade (not by local) on
  GAN's site. Trade-level only, so PARTIAL for any specific local.

## Scoring notes

Pass threshold commonly reported as 70%, but locals rank applicants by score
and fill from the top down, so passing is a floor rather than a goal. No
penalty for wrong answers, so guessing is never punished.

## What is unverified

- Per-section question counts and time limits for four of the six sections.
- Whether the battery is five or six sections, and the administered order.
- Whether every listed trade's Chicago local uses GAN, or only some.

**Consequence for the build:** exams built from this blueprint will use
per-section timers as required, but the timings for the four unconfirmed
sections are derived, not official. The site must show the PARTIAL badge and
say which numbers are derived. The best available resolution is the $20 GAN
study guide sold at application time, which is the only document likely to
state the real per-section structure.
