# Blueprint: Electrical (DuPage) — Electrical Training Alliance Aptitude Test

- **Test name / provider:** Electrical Industry Aptitude Test — Electrical Training Alliance (ETA; formerly NJATC)
- **Locals using it:** IBEW Local 701 / DuPage County JATC (Warrenville, IL). Same instrument is used by IBEW/NECA JATCs nationally.
- **Overall:** 69 questions / 97 minutes
- **Calculator:** NO (pencil and scrap paper provided)
- **Confidence:** CONFIRMED
- **Date checked:** 2026-09-21

## Section table

| # | Section | Questions | Time | Format | Calculator |
|---|---------|-----------|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Algebra and Functions | 33 | 46 min | Multiple choice | No |
| 2 | Reading Comprehension | 36 | 51 min | 4 passages, multiple choice | No |

Sections are separately timed and administered in this order. A short break
may be offered between sections.

## Scoring notes

Reported as a stanine, 1-9 (9 highest). Local 701 requires **4 or higher** to
be invited to interview. Final ranking combines the test score with an
interview score.
