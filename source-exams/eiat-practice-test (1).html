This exam follows the structure published in the official NEIEP EIAT study guide: cloze-style reading passages, diagram-based mechanical questions with three choices, and arithmetic questions with five choices where option (e) is No answer.

Every question here is original. Nothing is copied from the real exam. The formats, question counts and scoring are matched so the practice feels like the real thing.

How to take it

  • Work on paper. Do not use a calculator on Part 3.
  • Answer everything — there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so never leave a blank.
  • On Part 3, choose No answer only after you have worked the problem and none of the four numbers matches.
  • A section's timer cannot be paused, and you cannot return to a section once you leave it.

Timing

Timed mode allows 25 minutes for reading, 25 for mechanical and 40 for math. NEIEP does not publish official time limits, and locals set their own — this is the breakdown most preparation courses use. Treat it as realistic pressure, not gospel.
